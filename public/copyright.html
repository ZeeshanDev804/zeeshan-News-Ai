Copyright Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Purpose

ZEESHAN NEWS AI respects copyright and other intellectual property rights.

This Copyright Policy explains our general approach to third-party content, attribution, original value-added content, copyright concerns, and removal requests.

This policy describes our operational approach and is not a guarantee that copyright disputes or claims will never occur.

2. Third-Party News and Information

ZEESHAN NEWS AI may discover information from third-party publishers, RSS feeds, APIs, websites, public information sources, and other permitted sources.

Third-party publishers and rights holders retain their respective rights in material they own or control.

3. Original Value Addition

Our platform is designed to add value through activities such as:

  • news discovery and organization
  • original summaries
  • key-point extraction
  • categorization
  • contextual presentation
  • search and discovery features
  • AI-assisted analysis

We do not intend to present complete third-party articles as original ZEESHAN NEWS AI work.

4. Attribution

Where appropriate, we may identify the relevant publisher or source and provide a link to the original source.

Source attribution is intended to help readers identify where information originated.

Attribution does not transfer ownership of third-party copyrighted material to ZEESHAN NEWS AI.

5. Copyrighted Images, Videos and Media

Images, videos, graphics, logos, photographs, audio, and other media may be protected by copyright, trademark, licensing, privacy, or other rights.

Media used by the platform may therefore be subject to separate rights and permissions.

Where a rights concern is identified, the affected material may be restricted, replaced, unpublished, or removed while the matter is reviewed.

6. AI and Copyright

ZEESHAN NEWS AI may use artificial intelligence to assist with content processing, summarization, classification, similarity analysis, and copyright-risk signals.

AI systems are not legal authorities and an automated copyright-risk signal is not a final legal determination of infringement or ownership.

7. Duplicate and Similarity Detection

The platform may compare new content with existing material to identify potentially duplicated or substantially similar content.

Similarity detection may result in a content review, publication hold, restriction, or other internal action.

8. Copyright Complaint Process

If you believe that material available through ZEESHAN NEWS AI infringes your copyright or other applicable rights, you may submit a copyright complaint through the official contact channel provided by the service.

To help us review a complaint, please provide as much of the following information as reasonably possible:

  1. Your name or organization name.
  2. A description of the copyrighted work or other protected material.
  3. The URL or specific location of the material on ZEESHAN NEWS AI.
  4. A description explaining why you believe the material creates a rights concern.
  5. Information that can help establish your ownership or authorization to act on behalf of the rights holder.
  6. Your contact information so that the matter can be reviewed or clarified.

9. Review of Complaints

Copyright complaints may be reviewed by the appropriate team or responsible operator.

During review, we may take temporary measures where appropriate, including:

  • restricting access to material
  • holding publication
  • unpublishing content
  • removing media
  • replacing affected material
  • requesting additional information

10. Emergency Content Controls

Where a serious copyright, legal, safety, or content concern is identified, the platform may use emergency content controls to stop, restrict, or remove affected material.

Automated systems may also flag content for review when configured risk thresholds are reached.

11. Records and Audit Trail

Where supported by the platform, copyright complaints and related actions may be recorded for operational, security, compliance, and audit purposes.

Records may include relevant URLs, timestamps, content identifiers, source information, complaint details, review actions, and resolution information.

12. False or Abusive Complaints

Copyright complaints should be submitted honestly and with sufficient information to support the reported concern.

Knowingly false, fraudulent, abusive, or malicious reports may be rejected or handled through appropriate internal procedures.

13. Counter-Information and Clarification

Where appropriate, we may request additional information from the relevant rights holder, source, contributor, or other affected party before making a final content-management decision.

A content restriction or removal decision is an operational action and should not automatically be interpreted as a legal conclusion.

14. Source Policy and Licensing

ZEESHAN NEWS AI may maintain internal source policies identifying permitted sources, restrictions, attribution requirements, licensing considerations, or other content handling rules.

Source policies may be updated as the platform expands or as requirements change.

15. User-Submitted Material

If users are permitted to submit material to the platform, they are responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights or permissions to submit that material.

Users must not knowingly submit copyrighted material without appropriate authorization where authorization is required.

16. No Guarantee Against Claims

Although we aim to use responsible sourcing, attribution, originality checks, risk detection, and content controls, no automated or manual system can guarantee that a copyright complaint or legal claim will never occur.

Legitimate concerns will be handled through appropriate review and content-management procedures.

17. Policy Updates

This Copyright Policy may be updated as the website, content sources, technology, and applicable requirements evolve.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
