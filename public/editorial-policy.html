ZEESHAN NEWS AI

Editorial Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Our Editorial Purpose

ZEESHAN NEWS AI aims to provide useful, timely, clearly presented, and responsibly sourced news and information.

The platform uses technology and artificial intelligence to help discover, organize, analyze, summarize, and distribute information from available sources.

Our goal is to add original value through organization, analysis, summaries, context, and useful presentation rather than simply reproducing third-party publications.

2. News Sources

News may be discovered through RSS feeds, APIs, publisher websites, public information sources, and other permitted data sources.

Sources may differ in reliability, editorial standards, publication speed, and accuracy.

Source information should therefore be considered as part of the editorial evaluation process rather than as an automatic guarantee of truth.

3. Source Attribution

Where appropriate, ZEESHAN NEWS AI identifies or links to the original or relevant source of information.

Attribution does not mean that ZEESHAN NEWS AI adopts every statement, opinion, allegation, or conclusion contained in a third-party source.

4. Originality and Value Addition

We aim to create original presentation and value around information obtained from external sources.

  • Original summaries may be generated.
  • Key points may be extracted.
  • Related context may be provided.
  • Stories may be categorized and organized.
  • Headlines and metadata may be optimized for clarity and discovery.

We do not intend to republish complete third-party articles as our own work.

5. AI-Assisted Editorial Processing

Artificial intelligence may assist with editorial workflows including:

  • news discovery
  • duplicate detection
  • similarity analysis
  • headline processing
  • summaries
  • category classification
  • SEO metadata
  • content risk signals
  • quality checks

AI-generated output can contain mistakes, omissions, incorrect interpretations, or outdated information.

Important claims should therefore be reviewed against reliable sources before being treated as established fact.

6. Accuracy

We aim to identify and present information accurately and responsibly.

When information is developing or uncertain, appropriate wording may indicate that a report is preliminary, disputed, unconfirmed, or still developing.

We do not guarantee that every piece of information will always be complete or error-free.

7. Breaking and Developing News

Breaking news can change rapidly.

Early reports may later be corrected, expanded, contradicted, or withdrawn by original sources.

Automated systems may initially process developing information before later updates become available.

For significant developing stories, later information should be considered alongside earlier reports rather than assuming that the first published version is final.

8. Corrections and Updates

When a material error is identified, we may correct, update, replace, restrict, or remove the affected content.

Where appropriate, important corrections may be documented so that the editorial record remains understandable.

9. Duplicate and Similar Content

The platform may use automated similarity and duplicate detection to identify stories that substantially overlap with existing content.

Similarity detection is a technical signal, not a legal determination of copyright ownership or infringement.

10. Copyright Risk

Content may be reviewed for potential copyright or licensing concerns.

Where a material concern is detected, content may be flagged, restricted, held for review, or removed while the matter is evaluated.

Our copyright procedures are described in the Copyright Policy .

11. High-Risk Content

Certain topics require additional caution, including serious allegations, public safety, breaking emergencies, sensitive personal information, and potentially harmful claims.

Depending on the circumstances, automated publication may be restricted and content may require additional review before publication.

12. Opinions, Allegations and Claims

Statements made by individuals, organizations, governments, companies, or other sources may represent opinions, allegations, claims, or disputed assertions rather than independently established facts.

Editorial presentation should distinguish reported claims from verified information where practical.

13. Images, Videos and Other Media

Visual and multimedia material may be obtained from permitted sources or created through authorized systems.

Media may be subject to separate copyright, licensing, privacy, trademark, or platform requirements.

The platform may restrict or remove media when a rights, safety, policy, or quality concern is identified.

14. Editorial Independence

Editorial processing is intended to focus on relevance, source information, accuracy, originality, usefulness, and applicable editorial standards.

Automated systems may assist with processing, but automation does not by itself establish the truth or importance of a story.

15. Automated Publishing

ZEESHAN NEWS AI may use automated workflows for news discovery, processing, categorization, publishing, monitoring, and distribution.

Automated publishing may be subject to configurable risk controls, approval gates, content checks, and emergency controls.

16. User Feedback and Complaints

Users, publishers, rights holders, and other interested parties may report errors, copyright concerns, factual issues, or other content problems through the appropriate contact channel.

Reports may be reviewed and documented as part of the platform's editorial and content-management process.

17. Content Removal

Content may be restricted, unpublished, corrected, replaced, or removed when appropriate because of:

  • significant factual concerns
  • copyright or licensing concerns
  • privacy or safety concerns
  • policy violations
  • duplicate or erroneous publication
  • source withdrawal or correction

18. Editorial Review Records

Where supported by the system, important editorial actions may be recorded through audit logs or internal records.

Such records may include publication events, corrections, risk flags, content actions, source information, and review decisions.

19. Transparency

We aim to make the nature of our service clear: ZEESHAN NEWS AI is a technology-assisted news platform and is not the original publisher of every piece of information it references.

Readers should consult original sources when they need the full context, primary evidence, or authoritative version of a report.

20. Policy Updates

This Editorial Policy may be updated as the platform, technology, editorial workflow, or applicable requirements evolve.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
